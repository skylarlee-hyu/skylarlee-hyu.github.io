Leader vs. Firm: Does the News Frame Change Whether and How Investors Reason?

The same good news moves markets differently when it is told about a CEO rather than the company. This project asks whether leader-centered narratives change how much objective information investors seek before deciding, and how they use it. The next step tests the mechanism with fMRI.

Framing experiment KOSPI news panel · 2021–2025 fMRI · within-subject

Happy to share materials or talk collaboration: get in touch.
